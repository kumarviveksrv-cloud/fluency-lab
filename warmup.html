Morning Warm-Up
Step 1 of 4 ~5 min
Wake Up Your Mouth

Physical activation before any speaking. Takes 2 minutes.

Complete these in order:
Tongue rolls
Roll your tongue — 20 times
Lip trills
Lips relaxed, blow air — 20 times
Jaw drops
Open wide, release tension — 10 times
Vocal humming
Hum to activate cords — 30 seconds

Normal speed
